# Changelog

All notable changes to TeachLens are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-22 — Acorn

First public-ready release.

### Added

- English and Vietnamese interface, with English as the default.
- Green pointer highlight with click pulse.
- Circular per-monitor spotlight.
- Local per-monitor magnifier with circular and rounded-rectangle lenses.
- 1.5×, 2.0×, 2.5× and 3.0× zoom levels.
- Dynamic `Esc` handling and system-tray controls.
- Multi-monitor boundary and per-monitor DPI handling.
- Privacy, security, contribution and publishing documentation.
